# Nigeria Police Force (NPF) National Command Directory
## Territorial Formations, Zonal Commands, State Commands, Area Commands & Divisional Headquarters

Compiled from official public records of the **Nigeria Police Force (npf.gov.ng)**, the **Police Service Commission (PSC)**, and official State Police Command operational gazettes.

---

## 1. Apex Command: Force Headquarters (FHQ)

* **National Headquarters:** Louis Edet House, Shehu Shagari Way, Area 11, Garki, FCT Abuja.
* **Apex Leadership:** **Inspector-General of Police (IGP)**.
* **Departmental Directorate (Headed by Deputy Inspectors-General of Police - DIGs):**
  1. **Department of Operations ('B' Dept):** Tactical operations, election security, crowd control, PMF, CTU, EOD.
  2. **Department of Finance & Administration ('A' Dept):** Personnel, postings, discipline, budget, pensions.
  3. **Department of Logistics & Supply ('C' Dept):** Armament, vehicles, communications gear, uniform procurement.
  4. **Force Criminal Investigation Department ('D' Dept - FCID):** High-level criminal investigation, forensic lab, INTERPOL NCB.
  5. **Department of Training & Development ('E' Dept):** Police colleges, training academies, staff development.
  6. **Department of Research & Planning ('F' Dept):** Strategic planning, crime stats, organizational reform.
  7. **Department of Information & Communications Tech ('G' Dept - ICT):** Digital infrastructure, cybercrime, radio mesh, biometric databases.
  8. **Force Intelligence Bureau (FIB):** National intelligence collection, surveillance, counter-subversion.

## 6. Digital Integration with Election SitRep Platform

1. **User Identity & Jurisdiction Binding:**
   - Every officer in Election SitRep is assigned a verified NPF service number and territory level:
     - `national`: Force Headquarters oversight (IGP Portal).
     - `state`: State Command Situation Room (CP & Operations Department).
     - `area`: Area Command Tactical Hub (ACP Quick Reaction Force).
     - `division`: Divisional Police Officer (DPO).
     - `pu`: Polling Unit Ground Security Officer.
2. **Instant Emergency SOS Cascade:**
   - When a PU officer triggers the emergency SOS beacon on their mobile app, the alert instantly sounds across:
     - The Divisional Police Officer's dashboard.
     - The Area Command Quick Reaction Force (QRF) dispatch board.
     - The State and National Situation Room large-screen video walls.
3. **Evidence Archival:**
   - EC8A ballot tallies and incident logs are stamped with the official Division code and cryptographically signed into the tamper-proof ledger.
